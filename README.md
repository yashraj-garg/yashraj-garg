# Yashraj Garg

Final-year Psychological and Behavioural Sciences undergraduate at Trinity College, University of Cambridge, working across **statistical inference, computational neuroscience, and data-intensive research**.

My research interests centre on how statistical and computational models can be used to investigate cognition, individual differences, and human behaviour—particularly where conclusions depend on sampling, measurement, model specification, and the choice of statistical estimand.

## Current research

### Emotional memory and multimodal neuroimaging | University of Basel

I lead quantitative analyses and manuscript preparation for a first-author study of individual differences in emotional-memory enhancement.

My work includes:

- Statistical modelling of behavioural and neuroimaging data, including mixed-effects and regression approaches.
- Investigation of distinct sample definitions and their implications for statistical inference.
- Whole-brain and region-of-interest analyses, independent component analysis, and structural morphometry.
- Multiple-comparison correction, influence diagnostics, and sensitivity analyses.
- Reproducible analytical workflows using R and high-performance computing resources.

**Status:** First-author manuscript in preparation.

## Selected quantitative experience

**Statistical inference and modelling:** Regression, mixed-effects modelling, diagnostic assessment, statistical hypothesis testing, effect-size estimation, and sensitivity analyses across university coursework and empirical research.

**Mathematical modelling and dynamical systems:** Implementation and analysis of ordinary differential equation models, including numerical SIR simulations, equilibria, stability analysis, Jacobians, eigenvalues, nullclines, and phase-plane dynamics.

**Computational biology:** Sequence analysis, protein homology searches, multiple-sequence alignment, and phylogenetic inference.
